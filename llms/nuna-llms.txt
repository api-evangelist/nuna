# Nuna

> Nuna Incorporated is a San Francisco healthcare data and technology company (founded 2010, Jini Kim
> and David Chen). It built the first national Medicaid claims dataset and data pipeline for the U.S.
> Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, and today ships an AI-powered chronic-care coaching app
> delivered through health plans, employers and provider systems.

IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: Nuna has **no public API**. As of 2026-08-26 there is no developer portal, no
API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/Protobuf/WSDL contract, no SDK, no webhooks, no MCP server
and no agent card anywhere on Nuna's public surface. Do not attempt to construct calls against
api.nuna.com or developer.nuna.com — neither hostname resolves. Nuna's products are a consumer mobile
app and enterprise data services sold under contract. This file is generated by API Evangelist from a
third-party profile; it is not published by Nuna.

## Security, privacy and compliance
- [Trust](https://www.nuna.com/trust/): SOC 2 Type II audits (security, availability, confidentiality); HIPAA and HITECH including the Privacy, Security and Breach Notification Rules; BAAs with all vendors; AI governance aligned to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework; client data is never used to train any proprietary or third-party AI model.
- [Trust center](https://app.vanta.com/nuna.com/trust/048n92bu6ryb8pixhtfn3i): Vanta-hosted, self-service security documentation. Client-rendered — requires a browser.
- [Report a security issue](https://www.nuna.com/report-an-issue/): Coordinated vulnerability disclosure. Email vulnerabilities@nuna.com with a description, impact, and reproduction steps. No bug bounty, no stated SLA, no safe harbor.
- [Privacy policy](https://www.nuna.com/privacy/) (last updated 2026-03-11), [Terms of service](https://www.nuna.com/terms-of-service/) (last updated 2026-07-22), [Notice of privacy practices](https://www.nuna.com/notice-of-privacy-practices/), [Deletion policy](https://www.nuna.com/deletion-policy/).
